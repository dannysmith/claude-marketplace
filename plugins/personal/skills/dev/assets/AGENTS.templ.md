## Task Management

Tasks are markdown files: todo in `docs/tasks-todo/task-N-name.md` (lowest `N` is next up, a literal `x` means unprioritised), completed in `docs/tasks-done/task-YYYY-MM-DD-N-name.md` (the completion date), with the gitignored `docs/tasks-todo/temporary/` for working files.
