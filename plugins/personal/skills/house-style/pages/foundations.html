Foundations

Everything css/tokens.css defines: the palette, the semantic colours built on it, and the type, space and shape scales. The swatches read their values from the stylesheet, so this page can't drift from it.

Hues

Each step has a job. 500 is the hue itself; 300 and 400 are the light tints; 600 is for coloured text on light; 700 and 800 are the dark-mode border and background.

Greys

One cool grey. Slate is grey-800 and Charcoal is grey-950.

Beige

Warm neutrals for danny.is work. Switch the light background to Beige in the bar above to see them in use.

Semantic tokens

What components and pages actually use. These follow the mode and background switches.

Hue roles

Each hue as surface, border, text and vivid, adapting to the mode. Set data-color or data-status on an element and the --hue-* variables point at that hue.
