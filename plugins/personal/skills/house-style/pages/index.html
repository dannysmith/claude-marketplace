House style

Danny's default look for things that don't have one of their own: quick tools, small apps, slides and social images. These pages are the visual reference. The values live in css/ and the reasoning in references/.

Reference

Foundations

The palette, semantic colours, hue roles, type, space and shape. Everything in tokens.css.

Elements

Plain HTML under reset.css, tokens.css and base.css: text, buttons, form controls, dialogs.

Components

Optional pieces, one file each: callouts, fields, menus, the app shell and the rest.
